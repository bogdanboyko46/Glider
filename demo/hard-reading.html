14. Deposit Deductions and Dispute Procedure

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding subparagraph, and without prejudice to any remedy to which the Landlord would otherwise be entitled, the Tenant hereby acknowledges that the deposit, or such proportion thereof as the Landlord deems appropriate, may be withheld pending the adjudication of any dispute, whether or not that dispute has been formally lodged.

Deductions shall be proportionate to the dilapidation attributable to the Tenant, excluding deterioration attributable to ordinary wear; provided, however, that where the Tenant disputes the apportionment, the burden of substantiating that the dilapidation predated the tenancy shall be borne by the Tenant, who shall be obliged to do so through thorough photographic documentation, though the Landlord's inventory shall be presumed accurate throughout.
