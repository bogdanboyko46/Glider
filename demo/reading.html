How Plants Make Food

Photosynthesis is the process that plants use to convert light into chemical energy. Inside the chloroplast, chlorophyll absorbs approximately all of the red and blue light and subsequently reflects the green, which is the reason that leaves appear green to us.

The energy from that light splits water. The plant keeps what it needs and lets the oxygen go.

Carbon dioxide comes in through small openings under the leaf. The plant joins it with the hydrogen from water to build sugar, which feeds every part of it.

Some of that sugar is stored as starch in roots and seeds. When we eat a potato or a slice of bread, we are eating sunlight the plant saved for later.
