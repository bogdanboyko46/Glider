How plants make food

Photosynthesis is the process that plants use to convert light into chemical energy. Inside the chloroplast, chlorophyll absorbs approximately all of the red and blue light and subsequently reflects the green, which is the reason that leaves appear green to us.

The energy that is captured is then used to separate water molecules, and this releases oxygen as a by-product. Scientists are still studying whether their models can predict the effect that hotter summers will have on this process.

The plant stores that energy as sugar. It uses the sugar to grow new leaves, roots and seeds, and animals that eat the plant take in the same energy.
