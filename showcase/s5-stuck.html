en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photosynthesis
Photosynthesis is how plants turn light into food. It happens in tiny green parts of the leaf called chloroplasts, which catch the light of the sun. Inside the chloroplast, chlorophyll absorbs approximately all of the red and blue light and subsequently reflects the green, which is the reason that leaves appear green to us. The energy from that light splits water. The plant keeps what it needs and lets the oxygen go.
