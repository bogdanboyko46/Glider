How plants make food

Plants make their own food. They need light, water and air to do it. The work happens in the green parts of a leaf, and it is called photosynthesis.

Inside the chloroplast, chlorophyll absorbs approximately all of the red and blue light and subsequently reflects the green, which is the reason that leaves appear green to us.

The plant stores that energy as sugar. It uses the sugar to grow new leaves, roots and seeds.
