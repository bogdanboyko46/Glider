How plants make food

Photosynthesis is the process that plants use to convert light into chemical energy. Inside the chloroplast, chlorophyll absorbs approximately all of the red and blue light and subsequently reflects the green, which is the reason that leaves appear green to us. The energy that is captured is then used to separate water molecules.
