canonical_face_model.obj

What it is: the canonical 3D face mesh of MediaPipe Face Geometry (468 vertices, centimetres).
tests/bench/synth3d.mjs reads the vertex positions as the resting positions of the face
landmarks of its synthetic person. It is test data only: it is not part of the extension and is
not listed in glider-extension/manifest.json.
