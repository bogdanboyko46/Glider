Simplify hard text

When eye tracking notices you are stuck on a paragraph, Glider swaps it, right where it is, for a larger and simpler version. “Show original” puts it back.

Better rewrites with Claude optional

Without a key, Glider simplifies on this device: it swaps hard words for common ones and splits long sentences. With an Anthropic API key, Claude rewrites the paragraph properly a moment later.

Privacy: with a key saved, the paragraph you are stuck on is sent to Anthropic to be rewritten. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent without a key. The key is stored only in this browser.
